# Code of conduct

cfdi4j adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, and assume good faith. Criticize ideas, not people. Do not
harass anyone, publicly or privately, and do not publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer through
the contact method on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/alktrip). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer applies the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant:
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban.
